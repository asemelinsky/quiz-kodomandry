📘 Quick theory (advanced) — read before you start
1️⃣ Powers of negative numbers
(−a)²=+a²even power → POSITIVE
(−a)³=−a³odd power → keeps minus
(−a)⁴=+a⁴even → POSITIVE
−a²=−a²square 3 first, THEN negate
💡 Even power of (−a) → positive. Odd power → keeps the sign.
2️⃣ Multiple negatives multiplied
(−2) × (−3)=+62 negatives → +
(−2) × (−3) × (−4)=−243 negatives → −
(−2) × (−3) × (−4) × (−1)=+244 negatives → +
💡 Count the negatives. Even → positive. Odd → negative.
3️⃣ Nested brackets — innermost first
8 − [3 − (5 − 9)]=8 − [3 − (−4)] = 8 − 7 = 1
−2 × [5 − (7 − 9)]=−2 × [5 − (−2)] = −2 × 7 = −14
💡 Work inside-out. Don\'t skip layers. Re-write after each bracket.
4️⃣ Distribution with a negative
−3 × (2x − 5)=−6x + 15multiply BOTH terms, flip signs
−(x − 4)=−x + 4= 4 − x
a − b(x − c)=a − bx + bcdistribute −b carefully
💡 Minus distributes too. Every term inside brackets gets multiplied.
5️⃣ Equations with negative coefficients
−3x + 5 = 14→−3x = 9move +5 across as −5
then:−3x = 9→x = 9 ÷ (−3) = −3divide by negative
−(x − 4) = 9→−x + 4 = 9 → x = −5open brackets first
💡 Dividing both sides by a negative doesn\'t flip the "=" sign (no inequalities here) — but the sign of x changes carefully. Just do the arithmetic.
