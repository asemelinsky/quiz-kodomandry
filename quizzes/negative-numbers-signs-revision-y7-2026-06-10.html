1️⃣ Sign rules — multiplication & division
(+) × (+)=(+)
(−) × (−)=(+)
(+) × (−)=(−)
(−) × (+)=(−)
💡 Same signs → +. Different signs → −. Same rule applies to division.
2️⃣ Removing brackets (expanding)
+ (a + b)=a + bno change
+ (a − b)=a − bno change
− (a + b)=−a − b⚠️ flip BOTH signs
− (a − b)=−a + b = b − a⚠️ flip BOTH signs
k × (a + b)=ka + kbdistribute
−k × (a − b)=−ka + kbdistribute & flip
💡 If a minus sits in front of brackets — every sign inside flips.
3️⃣ Moving a term across the "=" sign
x + 5 = 12→x = 12 − 5 = 7+5 crosses, becomes −5
x − 3 = 8→x = 8 + 3 = 11−3 crosses, becomes +3
3x = 15→x = 15 ÷ 3 = 5× becomes ÷
x ÷ 4 = 6→x = 6 × 4 = 24÷ becomes ×
💡 Crossing the "=" sign — the operation flips to its opposite: + ↔ −, × ↔ ÷.
4️⃣ Powers of negative numbers
(−3)²=+9brackets first, then square — always positive
−3²=−9square the 3 first, then negate
−(3 − 5)²=−(−2)² = −4bracket → square → negate
💡 (−a)² = +a². −(a²) = −a². The brackets make all the difference.
5️⃣ Order of operations (BIDMAS)
B Brackets → I Indices/Powers → DM Divide & Multiply (left to right) → AS Add & Subtract (left to right)
💡 Don\'t skip ahead. Solve the deepest bracket first.
